# Standards and source register for the P4711 maintenance-support scenario

**Register ID:** DEMO-STD-047 | **Revision:** 1 | **Purpose:** Retrieval metadata and scope notes only  
**Important:** This file is not a standard, compliance determination, or work instruction. Confirm the edition adopted by the jurisdiction/site and consult the licensed/current controlled text before relying on requirements.

## Relevant references

| Reference | Relevance to the scenario | Limits |
|---|---|---|
| OSHA 29 CFR 1910.147, Control of hazardous energy (lockout/tagout) | A US regulatory reference concerning control of hazardous energy during covered servicing and maintenance. The official text addresses energy-control procedures, isolation devices, and stored/residual energy. | Applicability depends on jurisdiction and circumstances. It does not provide P4711-specific isolation points or authorize work. Use the employer's equipment-specific procedure and applicable local law. |
| API Standard 610, Centrifugal Pumps for Petroleum, Petrochemical and Natural Gas Industries | Potentially relevant to centrifugal pump design/specification where invoked by the purchase basis or owner requirements. API's public standards plan identifies the standard and edition information. | The sample does not establish pump type, API 610 applicability, contractual edition, or any particular requirement. Obtain the controlled standard and pump datasheet. |
| API Standard 682, Pumps—Shaft Sealing Systems for Centrifugal and Rotary Pumps | Potentially relevant to pump shaft-sealing systems and seal-support arrangements when applicable to the installed equipment/specification. | The sample does not establish seal type, plan, or edition. Do not infer seal repair steps or limits from the standard title. Obtain the OEM seal documentation and controlled standard. |
| ISO 14224:2016, Petroleum, petrochemical and natural gas industries — Collection and exchange of reliability and maintenance data for equipment | A framework for consistently collecting and exchanging reliability and maintenance data over equipment life. Useful for coding the mock work history and failure observations. | It is a data-collection reference, not a maintenance method, safe-work procedure, or P4711 technical limit. |
